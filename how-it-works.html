How It Works

From "we need help" to "we got this."

A simple process built around your student, not our schedule.

STEP 01

Free consultation

A 15-minute call or form submission. Tell us your student's grade, subjects, struggles, and goals: catching up, keeping up, or getting ahead.

STEP 02

The match

Within 2 to 3 days, we pair you with a vetted local tutor based on subject expertise, availability, location, and personality fit. You see their profile before anything is booked.

STEP 03

First session

Meet at your home, a nearby library, or online. The first session doubles as a diagnostic so your tutor builds a plan around real gaps, not guesses.

STEP 04

Progress you can see

After every session, parents get a short recap: what was covered, what's next, and how things are trending. Not clicking? We rematch free.
