About Us

Built in Houston, for Houston.

Founded by three friends who believe great tutoring shouldn't require a national call center or a stranger from an app.

Our Story

Why we started

Every Houston family searching for a tutor runs into the same two options: a national franchise that assigns whoever's available that week at franchise prices, or an app that hands you a stranger's profile and a rating you have no way to verify. We started Unbound Academics because neither one is actually built around your student.

Houston already has what's needed: graduate students, former teachers, and subject experts who are genuinely great at this. What was missing was a trusted local network that vets them properly, holds them accountable, and matches them to the right family instead of the next name on a list.

That's what "unbound" means to us: students unbound from the limits of a struggling class, and learning unbound from one-size-fits-all programs built for a franchise, not a kid.
