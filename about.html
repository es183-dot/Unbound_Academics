About Us

Built in Houston, for Houston.

Founded by three friends who believe great tutoring shouldn't require a national call center or a stranger from an app.

Our Story

Why we started

We started Unbound Academics because we kept seeing the same problem: Houston families choosing between expensive national tutoring franchises and anonymous online marketplaces where you never know who you're getting.

Houston is full of brilliant graduate students, former teachers, and subject experts who love teaching. What was missing was a trusted local network to connect them with families, with real vetting, real accountability, and real results.

That's what "unbound" means to us: students unbound from the limits of a struggling class, and learning unbound from one-size-fits-all programs.
